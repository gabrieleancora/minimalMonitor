## Implemented behavior

- Optional bounded, private secret-file loading; no token browser field,
  Debug/Serialize, database token or raw provider error reporting. Public
  Telegram trust roots and fixed `api.telegram.org:443` are independent of probe
  grants/custom roots. Whole-set public DNS policy rejects more than eight
  answers; at most two numeric connections are attempted before the single POST.
- Existing Hyper/rustls transport, scoped connection driver, no pool, redirects,
  compression or proxy environment routing. Ten-second total deadline, 32 KiB
  header/parser limits and 64 KiB response-body limit. Plain JSON text and
  disabled link previews; sanitized event snapshots contain names, fixed reasons
  and timestamps without target URLs/queries/IPs. Messages cap UTF-16 units at
  3000 and include UTC observations, stable incident IDs and dashboard link.
- Five-second digests preserve incident identity/order. Pending down/recovery
  combines into one dated resolved incident; an undelivered earlier alert is
  disclosed. An incident receipt bit remembers delivered down alerts after
  seven-day delivery metadata cleanup, including long/gapped incidents.
- One sender/independent slot, three-second destination pacing for alerts,
  retries/tests. Durable completion-plus-60-second transient retry. Shared-bot
  429 waits use max(60 seconds, provider wait), never wrap or reset on restart,
  settings or secret correction. A known 429 wait survives a stalled/failed body.
  Waiting consumes no attempt; permanent errors latch until correction.
- Durable request/acknowledgement sequence and bounded event references. A
  failed local acknowledgement keeps one receipt in memory and retries its
  persistence without another send. Restart preserves uncertain logical events
  with a 60-second retry wait. An admitted request reserves its attempt before
  transport; a crash can leave that attempted send uncertain. At-least-once
  delivery and possible duplicates remain explicit. Ten attempts/six-hour
  expiry stop further sends.
- Both 1000-pending-row and 2 MiB payload bounds retain incidents and existing
  jobs, count suppression, and enqueue a dated summary when capacity returns.
  Terminal receipts retain seven days after completion and at most 2000 rows;
  cleanup is bounded. Disabling/changing a destination or credential cancels old
  jobs with an audit record. An already submitted old-destination request cannot
  be recalled; its recipient is captured and never rewritten. Its provider
  cooldown still gates the bot after cancellation/pruning.
- Protected settings/test/receipt APIs, optimistic revisions, signed i64 chat
  validation, minute test-action throttle and bounded pagination. Read chat IDs
  are canonical decimal strings to preserve browser precision. Summary/settings
  expose separate delivery faults, missing token, oldest backlog/delay,
  suppression and acknowledgement-storage degradation; visual banners/forms
  remain WP-05.

### Correction and invariant

`Sender` now retains one notification-admission permit before awaiting Claim.
If that reply times out/is lost, the sender knows it has not entered transport
and keeps that bounded uncertainty state. Before any subsequent claim it sends
`ReconcileUnsubmittedClaim` to the same serialized SQLite worker. This acts as a
fence after the earlier operation: an uncommitted claim needs no change; a
committed reservation releases its still-sending rows and acknowledges its
request sequence atomically. No outstanding-request guard was removed.

Only the unsent reservation is refunded. Original expiry is enforced, terminal/
canceled jobs are never revived, and destination revisions, ordering, due times,
provider cooldowns and pacing remain intact. A repeated reconciliation after a
lost reconciliation reply sees the already-acknowledged sequence and does not
refund twice. The sender keeps admission and `persistence_degraded` until a
successful reconciliation reply confirms resolution. A later empty claim cannot
hide an outstanding unknown claim. The operation is only used by the admitted
sender before transport; known outcomes awaiting Finish and uncertain process
restart delivery retain their existing acknowledgement/duplicate semantics.

### Files and regression injection

- `src/notifications.rs`: retain the uncertain unsubmitted-claim permit, reconcile
  before proceeding, and update shared delivery health at the tick boundary.
- `src/storage/notifications.rs`: worker-ordered, idempotent reconciliation plus
  four actual-worker regression tests. A per-client one-shot test seam commits
  the real SQLite transaction, signals that fact, then withholds/drops the reply
  while the actual caller's Tokio three-second reply deadline expires. It is
  compiled only with `cfg(test)` and adds no production fault API or queue.
- `src/storage/mod.rs`: test-only per-client injection slot initialization.
- Notification/data-model/decision/acceptance contracts, README, planning status,
  worker/review follow-up and validation summaries: document recovery/evidence
  and leave independent acceptance pending. Prior working-tree changes and the
  original review's dated findings/verification were preserved.

Four focused tests in `storage::notifications::claim_reply_tests` pass on both
platforms:

1. `committed_claim_lost_reply_recovers_same_sender_and_keeps_health_degraded_until_fenced`:
   proves real committed `sending` state/unacknowledged sequence, no transport
   call before recovery, retained admission and visible health through another
   reconciliation timeout, then same-sender recovery without restart. The known
   unsent batch is submitted once and a subsequent test alert also delivers.
2. `lost_reconciliation_reply_is_idempotent_and_does_not_refund_an_attempt_twice`:
   loses the fence reply after its own commit, retains health/admission until
   confirmation, and demonstrates that a ninth-attempt reservation is refunded
   once before the real tenth submission.
3. `lost_claim_refunds_only_unsent_reservation_and_respects_expiry_and_tenth_attempt`:
   checks the last admissible attempt and exact six-hour expiry; an expired
   unknown reservation sends nothing and does not block a later alert.
4. `lost_claim_cancellation_does_not_redirect_jobs_or_clear_provider_cooldown`:
   cancels the reserved old destination, reconciles its outstanding sequence,
   retains the actual persisted 429 cooldown, and sends only the new job to its
   new configured chat after the appropriate waits.

The existing 17 notification integration tests remain unchanged and pass,
including actual >32 MiB pinned-WAL failed-Finish acknowledgement recovery,
incident ordering/resolved digests, permanent errors, settings/secret changes,
restart duplicate semantics, queue bounds and terminal retention. Together these
cover R-11/F-11 and R-05, V-11/V-04, and delivery-health requirements without
claiming exactly-once provider delivery.
